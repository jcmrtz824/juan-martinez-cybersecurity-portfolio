Fair questions

Do I have to change my number?

No. Your carrier forwards only the calls you don't answer. Everything else rings like it does today.

Is a robot talking to my customers?

An automated assistant sends the texts, and it says so if asked. It never quotes prices or promises a time. Its only job is to hold the customer and get you the details.

What if it gets something wrong?

You see every conversation. If the assistant can't answer, it tells the customer you'll follow up and texts you immediately.
