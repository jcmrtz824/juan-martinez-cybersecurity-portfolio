## Tool Usage Rules

- Use tools only in authorized lab environments or approved scopes.
- Do not test live third-party systems without written permission.
- Do not publish secrets, credentials, private client details, or sensitive infrastructure information.
- Document assumptions, scope limits, and evidence handling in every project.
