# About Me

I am a junior cybersecurity consultant developing practical skills in vulnerability management, security operations, incident response, threat intelligence, and risk assessment.

My focus is on helping organizations understand security risk in a clear, actionable way. I am interested in the full process of identifying weaknesses, validating findings, prioritizing risk, and communicating remediation steps that make sense for both technical teams and business decision-makers.

I have completed cybersecurity training through the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate and continue to build hands-on experience through TryHackMe labs and practical portfolio projects. These projects are designed as demonstrations and case studies unless otherwise noted.

My current areas of focus include:

- Threat Intelligence
- Vulnerability Management
- Security Operations
- Incident Response
- Risk Assessment

I approach cybersecurity work with a consulting mindset: understand the environment, identify the highest-risk issues, document evidence clearly, and recommend practical next steps. My goal is to continue building real-world capability through structured projects, hands-on labs, and professional reporting.

This portfolio represents my ongoing development as a cybersecurity professional and is intended to show my ability to analyze, document, and communicate security work at a professional standard.
