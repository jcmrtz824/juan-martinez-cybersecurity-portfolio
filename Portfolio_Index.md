# Portfolio Index

All projects in this portfolio are portfolio demonstrations or case studies unless otherwise noted. Each project is designed to demonstrate a specific cybersecurity consulting skill, including technical analysis, documentation, risk communication, and remediation planning.

## Projects

### [Project 01 - Vulnerability Assessment](./Project%2001%20-%20Vulnerability%20Assessment/)

Demonstrates a junior consultant's vulnerability assessment workflow, including asset review, scan planning, finding validation, severity prioritization, remediation guidance, and professional reporting.

Key skills demonstrated:

- Vulnerability management
- Risk assessment
- Technical evidence collection
- Remediation planning
- Client-ready reporting

### [Project 02 - Threat Intelligence Report](./Project%2002%20-%20Threat%20Intelligence%20Report/)

Demonstrates public-source threat intelligence reporting for CVE-2025-53770, including executive risk rating, CVSS analysis, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, detection opportunities, documented public IOCs, defensive recommendations, and patch guidance.

Key skills demonstrated:

- Threat intelligence research
- Public source validation
- CVSS analysis
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping
- Detection and remediation planning
- Executive risk communication
