## Reporting Standards

Reports should follow the master style guide:

- Use honest portfolio demonstration language
- Avoid invented client, employer, or production claims
- Keep executive summaries concise
- Separate technical severity from business priority
- Use references for standards, frameworks, and scoring decisions
- Include sanitized evidence only
