# The Five Numbers

Ten minutes every Sunday. This is the boring one, and it's what makes everything else improvable instead of guesswork.

---

## The five

### 1. Code-word redemptions
Tally marks at the register when somebody says the word from that week's post.

**This is your only hard number connecting content to sales.** Everything else about content is inference; this is people who walked in because of a video and told you so. Setup in [04-TURN-VIEWS-INTO-VISITS.md](./04-TURN-VIEWS-INTO-VISITS.md).

### 2. "How did you hear about us?"
Four buckets: **Google / TikTok / drove by / friend.**

Ask new faces. Thirty seconds a day. After a month you'll know whether to put your hour into filming or into Google — and right now you're guessing.

### 3. Michelada attach rate
`micheladas ÷ total beers × 100`

Your highest-margin lever. If this number drops, somebody stopped asking. Each 10 points on 100 beers/week is about **+$1,200/year**. See [05-SELL-MORE-PER-TICKET.md](./05-SELL-MORE-PER-TICKET.md).

### 4. Average ticket
`total sales ÷ number of tickets`

The number every upsell is trying to move. Elotes, combos, micheladas all show up here.

### 5. Your median video views
Look at your last 10 posts. Take the middle one.

Not the average — the **median**, because one viral video distorts an average badly. Knowing your median is what lets you spot an outlier: when a video does 3× it, that's your signal to make three more like it immediately. The whole method in [01-CONTENT-PLAYBOOK.md](./01-CONTENT-PLAYBOOK.md) depends on knowing this number, and most accounts never check it.

## The sheet

One row a week, in a notebook or a spreadsheet.

```
Week of: __________

 1. Code-word redemptions ................. ______
 2. Heard about us:  Google ____  TikTok ____  Drove by ____  Friend ____
 3. Micheladas ____ / Beers ____  = ______%
 4. Average ticket ........................ $______
 5. Median video views (last 10) .......... ______

 Total sales this week .................... $______
 Posts published this week ................ ______   (target: 5-7)
 Best video this week ..................... ____________ views
 Food % of sales .......................... ______%   (only matters for later/)

 One thing I noticed: ____________________________________________
```

That last line earns its keep. *"Nobody ordered elotes on Tuesday because the card fell off the table"* is the kind of thing that never makes it into a number but costs you money every week.

---

## Monthly review, 20 minutes

First Sunday of the month:

1. **Which channel is bringing people?** Google or TikTok. Put next month's hours there.
2. **What were your top 3 videos?** What did they have in common — format, dish, time of day? **Make more of those.**
3. **What's your median now** vs. last month? Rising median means the account is compounding.
4. **Is the michelada attach rate moving?** If not, the ask isn't happening. Retrain, don't re-plan.
5. **Average ticket up?** If not, which upsell isn't being offered?
6. **Replace one guess with a fact.** Pull your real beer and lime invoice prices and correct the numbers in [05-SELL-MORE-PER-TICKET.md](./05-SELL-MORE-PER-TICKET.md). Takes 20 minutes and makes every table in these docs yours instead of mine.

## What good looks like after 90 days

| Number | Target by end of month 3 |
|---|---|
| Posts per week | 5–7, consistently |
| Median video views | up 3× from month 1 |
| % of views from Atlanta metro | 40%+ |
| Code-word redemptions | 15+/week |
| Michelada attach rate | 40%+ |
| Google reviews | +30, rated 4.5★ or better |
| Average ticket | up 10% |
| Added profit from upsells alone | **+$200/week** |

That last row is reachable without a single new customer — it's the three sentences in [05-SELL-MORE-PER-TICKET.md](./05-SELL-MORE-PER-TICKET.md). The content work is what adds new customers on top.

## If the numbers aren't moving

| Symptom | Most likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Views are flat | Not enough posts, or not local | Post daily; tag the location; say "Kennesaw" out loud |
| Views fine, nobody comes in | No address, no specific dish, no urgency | [04-TURN-VIEWS-INTO-VISITS.md](./04-TURN-VIEWS-INTO-VISITS.md) |
| People come in, ticket is flat | Nobody's asking | The seven sentences in [05-SELL-MORE-PER-TICKET.md](./05-SELL-MORE-PER-TICKET.md) |
| Nothing is moving at all | Too early, or you stopped | Weeks 1–4 always look like failure. Keep posting. |

That last row matters most. The outlier data is unambiguous: the breakouts came from small accounts that kept posting until one landed. **Quitting in week three is the only way to guarantee this doesn't work.**
