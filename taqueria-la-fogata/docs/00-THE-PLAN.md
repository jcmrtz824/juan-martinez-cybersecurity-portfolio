# The Plan: Raise Sales at the Taqueria

**Taqueria La Fogata — Kennesaw, GA**

The goal right now is **more sales**: more people coming by, more of them trying the micheladas, the birria tacos, the elotes, the whole menu.

Liquor and the patio are still the long-term plan. They are **not** the priority, and they've been moved to [`later/`](./later/) so they stay out of the way until you want them.

---

## What you already have

You're a full taqueria, running. You have the beer license. **You already sell micheladas.** The food is already good and people already come at peak.

So this is not a startup plan and it isn't a fix-the-business plan. It's a **demand plan**: get more people through the door, and get the ones already there to try more.

---

## The three levers, in order of return

### Lever 1 — Be findable the moment somebody is hungry

Most of your potential customers aren't deciding between you and another taqueria. They're typing "tacos near me" into Google Maps and picking from what appears. If you're not in that list, you don't exist for that decision.

This is free, it takes an afternoon, and it's the highest-return thing on this page. → **[03-GOOGLE-BUSINESS-PROFILE.md](./03-GOOGLE-BUSINESS-PROFILE.md)**

### Lever 2 — Content that reaches people in Cobb County

This is what you asked for, and it's the engine. The research says something genuinely encouraging: **follower count is not what decides whether a restaurant video breaks out.** The biggest breakouts in the data came from the *smallest* accounts.

→ **[01-CONTENT-PLAYBOOK.md](./01-CONTENT-PLAYBOOK.md)** — what actually works, from real outlier data
→ **[02-30-DAY-SHOT-LIST.md](./02-30-DAY-SHOT-LIST.md)** — 30 days of videos, already specified

### Lever 3 — Turn views and visits into bigger tickets

A view that doesn't become a visit is worth nothing, and most restaurant content dies in that gap. And a customer who comes in and orders three tacos is worth half of one who orders three tacos, an elote and a michelada.

## The weekly rhythm

The whole plan is four hours a week, and most of it is filming.

| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Slow afternoon** | Batch-film. 8–12 clips. That's two weeks of posts. | 45 min |
| **Daily** | Post one clip. Reply to every comment in the first hour. | 15 min |
| **Once a week** | Google Business Profile: 3 photos, 1 post, reply to reviews | 15 min |
| **Sunday** | Write down the week's numbers | 10 min |

If you only ever do the first two rows, you'll still be ahead of nearly every restaurant in Kennesaw — because almost none of them post consistently, and consistency is most of the advantage.

---

## What to expect, honestly

Content is not a switch. Here's the realistic shape of it:

**Weeks 1–4.** Views are small. This feels like it isn't working. It is — you're building a library and teaching the algorithm what your account is about. The job in this phase is **not quitting**.

**Weeks 4–8.** One video does noticeably better than the rest. That video is the most important piece of information you'll get. Make three more exactly like it.

**Weeks 8–12.** You start hearing "I saw you on TikTok" at the register. That's the number that matters. Write it down every time.

**After that.** The breakout. It may be month 2 or month 7, and nobody can tell you which — but the data is clear that it's much more about volume and consistency than follower count.

What this plan will **not** do is produce a line out the door next Tuesday. What it reliably does is compound.

## One honest correction

An earlier version of this plan told you to launch micheladas as a quick win. You already sell them — my mistake, and the michelada math is now where it belongs: in [05-SELL-MORE-PER-TICKET.md](./05-SELL-MORE-PER-TICKET.md), about selling **more** of them, which is a different and better problem.

The margin fact still stands and it's worth keeping in your head: a $5 beer makes you about $4.08. The same beer as an $8 michelada makes about **$6.40**. Every beer you convert is **+$2.32** for roughly 68¢ of mixers. The question isn't whether to sell micheladas. It's what your **attach rate** is — what fraction of beers go out as micheladas — and whether anyone is asking.
