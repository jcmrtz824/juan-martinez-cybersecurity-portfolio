# The Next 90 Days: Money Without a License

Summer 2027 is 20 months away. This document is about what you earn **between now and then**, with no license, no permit, and almost no money.

These six moves cost under $500 combined. They also de-risk the 2027 plan, because each one tests an assumption you'd otherwise be betting $40,000 on.

---

## Move 1 — Launch micheladas (do this first)

**Cost: ~$60 of inventory. Time: an afternoon.**
**Gate: confirm with the Business License Office first — question #1 in [02-GEORGIA-LIQUOR-LICENSE.md](./02-GEORGIA-LIQUOR-LICENSE.md).**

A michelada is beer plus non-alcoholic mixers, so it very likely sits inside the beer license you already hold.

**What you need:** Clamato (or tomato juice), limes, hot sauce, Worcestershire, Maggi, Tajín, 16 oz cups or pint glasses.

**What it earns:** a $5 beer (profit $4.08) becomes an $8 michelada (profit $6.40). **+$2.32 per beer for 68 cents of mixers.**

**How to run it:**

- **Three versions, no more.** Michelada clásica $8. Michelada preparada — Tajín rim, cucumber, extra spice — $9. Chelada — just beer, lime, salt — $7.
- **One printed card per table and one at the register.** Not a line buried in the menu. A photo, three options, three prices.
- **Train one sentence:** *"¿Quiere que se la prepare como michelada?"* / *"Want me to make that a michelada?"* — asked every single time someone orders a beer. That sentence is the entire marketing plan.
- **Make the first one you serve each day look perfect** and photograph it. That's a week of TikTok and Google Posts content.

**Why it matters beyond the money:** this is your live experiment in whether your customers will pay for drinks. If micheladas sell, the margarita plan is validated by real behavior from real customers. If they don't sell at $8, you need to know that before you spend $40,000 on a bar.

## Move 2 — Claim and build out your Google Business Profile

**Cost: $0. Time: 2 hours, then 10 minutes a week.**

This is the highest-return free action available to any local restaurant, and you don't have it yet.

When somebody in Kennesaw opens Maps and types "tacos near me," what shows up is Google Business Profiles. Not websites, not TikTok. If you're not there, or you're there with no hours and two bad photos, you're invisible at the exact moment somebody is deciding where to eat in the next fifteen minutes.

Full step-by-step in **[06-GOOGLE-BUSINESS-PROFILE.md](./06-GOOGLE-BUSINESS-PROFILE.md)**. If you do only one thing from this entire folder, do this one.

**Cost: $0 on GitHub Pages, or ~$12/year if you buy a domain.**

There's a complete, working, mobile-first website in [`../website/`](../website/). You edit one file — `site-config.js` — with your real phone, address, hours and menu, and it's done.

## Move 4 — Start the TikTok engine properly

**Cost: $0. Time: 30 minutes of filming a week.**

You already have TikTok, which puts you ahead. The problem with most restaurant accounts is they post when someone remembers to, which means never.

The fix: **film in batches, post daily.** One 30-minute session on your slowest afternoon produces two weeks of content. Food filmed up close, with sound, in natural light, needs no editing skill — the griddle does the work.

90 days of specific videos, with hooks written in Spanish and English, in **[05-CONTENT-ENGINE.md](./05-CONTENT-ENGINE.md)**.

**The one thing to understand:** for a restaurant, TikTok views are not the goal. A video with 40,000 views from people in Ohio is worth less than one with 900 views from people in Cobb County. Everything in the content plan is built to be **locally** discoverable — tagging Kennesaw, naming nearby landmarks, using the on-screen text people actually search.

## Move 5 — Build a happy hour with the menu you already have

**Cost: $0. This is your dead-hours dress rehearsal.**

You don't need liquor to test whether people will come at 4pm. You need a reason.

Pick a window — say **Mon–Thu, 3–6pm** — and make it an event:

- $1 off all beer and micheladas
- A snack priced to pull people in: 2 tacos for $5, or free chips and salsa with any drink
- Name it. "La Hora Feliz." Put it on a sandwich board on the sidewalk, on Google Posts, on TikTok, and on the website.

**Then count what happens.** Covers in that window, before and after. That number is the single most important input to the entire patio decision, and right now you don't have it. Two months of counting turns the patio from a hope into a forecast.

If happy hour moves the needle *without* liquor and *without* a patio, the 2027 plan is going to work. If nobody comes even with cheap beer and cheap tacos, you've learned something crucial for $0 instead of $40,000.

## Move 6 — Start the numbers habit

**Cost: $0. Time: 10 minutes every Sunday.**

Six numbers, once a week, in a notebook or a spreadsheet. Details in [08-TRACKING.md](./08-TRACKING.md).

This is the least exciting item here and probably the most valuable. In March 2027, when you're deciding whether to spend $15,000 or $35,000 on the patio, the difference between a good decision and a guess is whether you have six months of your own data. The CPA affidavit for the liquor license also needs your food-vs-alcohol split — so you're going to be tracking this anyway. Starting now just means it's ready.

## The 90-day schedule

### Month 1 — October 2026
- [ ] Make Call 1 to the Business License Office. Get the michelada answer and the real license fee.
- [ ] Check felony/probation eligibility for everyone who'll be on the license.
- [ ] Claim and fully build out the Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Launch micheladas (once the city confirms).
- [ ] Start the weekly numbers sheet.

### Month 2 — November 2026
- [ ] Put the website live. Link it on the Google profile and in the TikTok bio.
- [ ] Launch "La Hora Feliz" 3–6pm Mon–Thu. Sandwich board out front.
- [ ] First batch-film session. Post daily.
- [ ] Calls 2 and 3 — Zoning and Building Services. Confirm the patio is permittable and **that parking works**.
- [ ] Call 5 — insurance. Get the dram shop quote.
- [ ] Ask every customer who's leaving happy for a Google review. Target 20 new ones this month.

### Month 3 — December 2026
- [ ] Review the numbers. Did micheladas sell? Did happy hour move 3–6pm?
- [ ] **Decision point:** do the real figures still support the patio? Revise the budget up or down.
- [ ] Get contractor quotes for the patio. Get at least three.
- [ ] Engage a CPA who has filed a Kennesaw alcohol affidavit.
- [ ] Holiday content push — tamales, posadas, Nochebuena. Your highest-engagement season of the year.
