# 30 Days of Videos

Thirty posts, already decided. No thinking required — just film and post.

**How to use this:** four batch-film sessions, one a week, 45 minutes each. Each session covers the next 7–8 days. Film the whole week's clips in one go while the griddle is hot.

Every caption uses the same block from [01-CONTENT-PLAYBOOK.md](./01-CONTENT-PLAYBOOK.md) — address, hours, phone, local hashtags. Only the first line changes, and it's written out for you below.

**Rules that apply to all 30:** vertical, sound on, phone ~12 inches away, natural light, open mid-action, on-screen text in the first second, trending regional Mexican audio, tag the location.

## Film session checklist

Tape this by the door.

- [ ] Phone charged, storage free, **lens wiped** (this one ruins more clips than anything else)
- [ ] Cheap tripod or a propped-up box
- [ ] Shooting near the door or outside in shade — never under fluorescents alone
- [ ] Griddle hot, cheese out, limes cut, Tajín and Clamato ready
- [ ] Clean plates, clean counter, nothing in the background you don't want on camera
- [ ] Vertical. **Check this every time.**
- [ ] Film each shot **three times** — you'll want the third one
- [ ] Open mid-action. The food is already moving when you hit record.

## Day 31 and beyond

Don't write a new plan. Do this:

1. Open your analytics. Find your **top three** videos of the month.
2. Work out your **median** views across all 30.
3. Any video that beat your median by **3× or more** — make three more of those.
4. Re-run this list with the winners swapped in for the losers.

That's the whole system. Post, find what works, make more of it. The data says the breakout comes from volume and consistency, not from cleverness.
